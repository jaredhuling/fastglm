Partial ordinal throughput: development evidence

Generated by benchmarks/ordinal-partial-throughput/summarize.R.
Baseline: commit 17c4a8a, package version 0.1.3.

Purpose and candidate
---------------------
The candidate reuses current.predictor_step when bounding current.step for
the first scoring search. That value was computed for exactly that vector
during the current evaluation. Fallback directions still receive their own
scan, and the scaled direction receives the unchanged feasibility check.
The likelihood, bias adjustment, information factorization, convergence
criteria, and compensated predictor/gap arithmetic are unchanged.

Limits and next decision
------------------------
These are bounded deterministic performance checks, not coverage evidence.
All timed cases have positive grouped category counts and regular interior
fits. Sparse boundary failures remain in the separate boundary study.
Runs used one OpenMP/OpenBLAS/vecLib thread and separate R processes, with
baseline preceding current. Three whole-fit repetitions, timing resolution,
thermal effects, and fixed process order limit small timing comparisons.
The benchmark reader references an in-memory fixture, so the results do not
measure external-storage latency or establish a peak-process-memory gain.
The source improvement removes an entire repeatable read without adding
retained observations. It is a computational reuse, not a changed estimator.
Larger rowwise kernels and source callback costs are better-supported next
profiling targets than an immediate information-factorization rewrite.

The initial scan-budget regression counted two preprocessing passes; the
source actually checks frequencies, scaling, and rank in three passes.
Only that test accounting was corrected; no production or numerical
acceptance rule changed in response to the test failure.
