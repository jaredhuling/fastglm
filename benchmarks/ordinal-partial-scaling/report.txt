--------------------

The 0.05/0.10 ms common-logit evaluation comparison is too close to
timer granularity to interpret its twofold ratio as a regression. The
whole-fit comparisons show no material slowdown in these small cases.
Five repetitions, fixed process order, and timer resolution limit the
precision; differences of a few percent should not be treated as gains.

Decision and remaining limits
-----------------------------
Keep the current dense factorization. Dimensions through 74 parameters
are fast in this bounded study, and the profile cannot identify a solver
bottleneck. A useful next development experiment would profile larger
dimensions at native-operation granularity and separately measure source
callback/pass costs before changing the implementation.
Sparse individual-outcome data can still approach an ordering boundary
without a converged meanBR root. The retained coded-response regression
has the same explicit failures under baseline and current native code;
it remains outside the successful timing grid and does not receive
covariance-based inference. No convergence criterion or estimator was
changed to improve these results.
