Certified boundary case: saturated fully varying cumulative-logit models
2026-11-04; derivation before the fresh numerical confirmation

Exact scope
-----------
Assume logit mean bias reduction, no common-slope columns, q=K-1>=2, and
G=p_n+1 distinct positive-frequency varying-predictor patterns z_g. Require
the square pattern matrix X_* with rows (1,-z_g') to be invertible, and each
pattern to have one fixed offset o_g. Independent frequency observations at a
pattern pool into multinomial counts y_g1,...,y_gK with positive total m_g.
Zero-frequency rows impose neither a likelihood contribution nor a constraint.

The original parameters at threshold j are (alpha_j,gamma_j). Their predictors
satisfy eta_.j = X_* (alpha_j,gamma_j)' - o. Because X_* is invertible, the
collection of all eta_gj is an invertible affine change of the original
parameters. This is exact model saturation at the observed patterns: each
pattern has its own q unconstrained cumulative predictors, subject only to
ordering within that pattern. The full likelihood factors by pattern.

Mean-bias reduction is equivariant under this affine change: its Jacobian is
constant, the coordinate-map Hessian is zero, and the full adjustment transforms
like the likelihood score. Thus the full original adjusted equations are
equivalent to independent saturated cumulative-logit adjusted equations for
each pattern. This is not bias reduction after a nonlinear probability or
log-gap reparameterization.

Single-pattern derivation
-------------------------
Write F_j=logit^(-1)(eta_j), P_j=F_j-F_(j-1), F_0=0, F_K=1, and
g_j=F_j(1-F_j). In a positive multinomial model the diagonal of inverse
expected information for eta is

 V_jj = F_j(1-F_j)/(m*g_j^2).

The cumulative mean-bias correction is therefore

 c_j = (m/2) g'_j V_jj = (1-2F_j)/2 = 1/2-F_j,
 c_0=c_K=0.

Consequently a_k=c_k-c_(k-1) equals 1/2-P_k at the two endpoints and -P_k
at every intermediate category. In the adjusted score,

 U* = sum_k grad(P_k) (y_k+a_k)/P_k,

the contribution -sum_k grad(P_k) is zero. These equations are exactly the
ordinary multinomial likelihood scores for pseudo-counts

 v=(y_1+1/2, y_2,...,y_(K-1), y_K+1/2).

Their unique maximum on the closed probability simplex is
 P_k=v_k/(m+1), and cumulative probabilities are
 F_j=(sum_(k<=j)y_k+1/2)/(m+1).
The map from ordered finite eta to the strictly positive simplex is bijective
with nonsingular derivative. An interior adjusted-score root must therefore
have those probabilities. If any intermediate count is zero, its probability
must be zero and the two adjacent cumulative predictors coincide. No root
exists in the strict positive-probability domain. Conversely, when every
intermediate count is positive, the displayed probabilities give its unique
interior root; endpoint counts may be zero because their pseudo-counts are
positive. Boundary thresholds remain finite, but ordinary interior covariance
and inference cannot be inferred from their finiteness.

Concrete two-pattern example
-----------------------------
Take z=0 and 1, offsets -.2 and .3, and count rows (3,0,2,4) and (1,2,0,5).
Every category is represented globally. The probability targets are
(.35,0,.20,.45) and (1.5,2,0,5.5)/9. Within the first pattern the first two
cumulative probabilities coincide; within the second the last two coincide.
The affine map back to alpha/gamma is invertible and finite, so this gives
an exact example where all global categories are represented but the requested
strictly positive mean-bias-reduced model has no solution.

Observable bounded-memory certificate
-------------------------------------
The proposed helper examines only logit meanBR with no common predictors.
It tracks at most p_n+1 exact varying-predictor patterns, their offsets and
pooled counts. A further distinct pattern or an offset difference within a
pattern disables the certificate. It retains O(p_n^2+p_n*K) values and no row
arrays. Add-block temporary work is proportional to the current input block.
After the existing data pass, exact pattern count and a conservative full-rank
check in anchored/scaled coordinates establish the saturated structure. An
empty pooled intermediate category then supplies the mathematical certificate.
Approximate pattern or offset matching is prohibited. Near-singular pattern
designs are left uncertified. This numerical rank check is the same kind of
finite-precision identification check used elsewhere, not formal exact rank
arithmetic over arbitrary real input.

Early failure is preferable to returning a boundary estimate as if it had a
regular covariance. The proposed message explains that this fully flexible
logit model has no mean-bias-reduced solution with all probabilities positive
because an intermediate category is absent within a predictor pattern.
It does not silently pool categories, clip probabilities, or change the
estimator. Other links, common slopes, more patterns, nonconstant within-pattern
offsets and the original sparse 96-row fixture remain outside the certificate.
Failed independent optimizations do not prove root absence for those models.

Primary source and applicability
--------------------------------
Kosmidis (2014), Improved estimation in cumulative link models, JRSS B 76,
169-196, DOI 10.1111/rssb.12025, equations (6)-(9), Section 5.1 and Section 6.1,
pages 176-177. These sections were read in the published cached full text.
Section 6.1 derives the cumulative-logit endpoint-half-count adjustment;
Section 5.1 explains why generic adjusted counts can be negative and why
ordinary likelihood concavity cannot be assumed for them. The saturated
pattern reduction and bounded observable certificate above specialize the
established adjustment using an explicit invertible affine map. They make no
new estimator, universal finiteness, or higher-order inference claim.
