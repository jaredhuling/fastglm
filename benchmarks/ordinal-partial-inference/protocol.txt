Frozen 2026-11-04 after development equation checks and before this confirmation

Use the eighteen-row fixture in confirm.R, distinct from development and unit
test data. All four categories are represented; two predictors are nonparallel,
one is common, offsets are nonzero, and integer frequency weights include zeros.
Run logit, probit and complementary log-log. No random inference simulation,
rejection-rate calibration, timing comparison or tuning to results is included.

Check a two-degree-of-freedom hypothesis equating z1 slopes across thresholds
while z2 remains nonparallel. For ML, compare its constrained likelihood with
a reduced native model and compare both full/reduced likelihoods and all-row
probabilities with ordinal::clm nominal-effect fits. Likelihood tolerance 2e-7,
probability tolerance 2e-6, likelihood-ratio tolerance 2e-7.

For ML and full-model meanBR score tests, recompute joint category log-score
derivatives, Hessians and the generic cumulant adjustment using numDeriv. Check
the returned nuisance equations (standardized tolerance 5e-7), efficient
quadratic (absolute tolerance 2e-5), and linear constraints (2e-11). Verify
invariance under nonsingular changes of constraint row basis (2e-7).

Compute an 80% ML profile interval for a specified threshold-specific coefficient.
Independently maximize the ordinary likelihood using nlminb at both endpoints,
with that coefficient fixed. Check constrained log likelihood at 2e-7 and the
likelihood-ratio endpoint target at 3e-5. Require independent convergence.

Expanded integer-frequency rows must reproduce both LR and adjusted-score
statistics within 2e-7, and identical offset semantics. Failures, including a
failed external optimizer, are retained. The runner writes comparisons as they
are completed, refuses existing output, and preserves session/source hashes.
No tolerance or hypothesis changes after viewing these results.

Unit tests separately cover observed-domain validity with unordered raw
thresholds, infeasible row-gap constraints, zero-frequency extrapolation rows,
failed iteration limits, unrepresentable affine constraints, and compensated
extreme-covariate arithmetic. Their fixtures are development regressions, not
the fresh confirmation dataset.
