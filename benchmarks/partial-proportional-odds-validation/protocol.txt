Partial proportional odds: independent numerical confirmation
Protocol fixed before evaluating the new native implementation, 2026-11-04

Role and separation
-------------------
This is a deterministic confirmation of equations and transformations, not a
simulation calibration or a timing study. The earlier prototype and its wine
rounding discrepancy are development evidence and remain unchanged. The two
fixtures in reference.R differ from that prototype and were fixed before this
confirmation. No rejection probabilities or coverage thresholds are tuned.

reference.R obtains single-category log-probability derivatives using numDeriv.
It constructs the general expected-cumulant mean-bias adjustment, independently
of the native cumulative adjusted-count implementation. Probabilities are kept
away from machine-precision tails because numerical differentiation is the
reference, not a test of tail arithmetic. Native tail and feasibility tests are
separate. Group counts, integer frequency weights, zero-weight observations,
and nonzero offsets are present in both fixtures.

Fixed comparisons and absolute maximum-error tolerances
-------------------------------------------------------
For each of logit, probit, and complementary log-log, and each fixture:
 ordinary score versus numerical category derivatives: 2e-6
 expected information versus score outer products: 2e-6
 expected information versus expected negative Hessian: 2e-6
 mean-bias adjustment versus generic P_t + Q_t formula: 2e-5
 covariance versus inverse numerical information: 2e-6
 log likelihood versus direct category probabilities: 1e-10

All links additionally check the common-slope reduction, binary reduction,
grouped-frequency expansion with offsets, and centered/scaled predictor
recoding at tolerance 2e-9. A nonorthogonal baseline-plus-deviation map checks
the generic adjustment's linear equivariance at tolerance 2e-5. A multiplied
frequency sample must multiply information and ordinary score, leaving the
adjustment unchanged (2e-9). Zero-frequency rows outside the probability domain
must not change the fit state; positive-frequency invalid rows must be rejected.

External wine example
---------------------
The author's unmodified supplementary bpolr.R implementation is sourced from an
external cache, with its dependencies in a temporary library. Refit the published
model with its stated original coordinates, recording estimates at full
precision. Compare native coefficients and covariance with this independently
maintained solver at tolerance 2e-6, and require native standardized adjusted
score and remaining predictor update below 1e-8. Printed Table 6 is not used as
a high-precision numerical target. Any discrepancy is retained and investigated.

Fresh fitted-root comparison, fixed before the first native confirmation run
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Refit both fixed fixtures by ML and mean bias reduction for all three links.
Use the original bpolr implementation on frequency-expanded rows with the same
offsets and compare coefficients and covariance at 2e-6. Require native adjusted
(or ordinary for ML) standardized scores and remaining predictor updates below
1e-8 and record both solvers' convergence indicators. These comparisons address
implementation at roots in addition to the prespecified pointwise equations.

Outcome handling
----------------
The runner refuses to replace existing output. Record every comparison, source
hashes, session details and native fitted-root diagnostics in a fresh directory.
Failures cannot be removed or tolerances changed after seeing their values.
An implementation correction requires a separately identified rerun. These
checks validate equations and selected numerical roots; they do not establish
universal root existence, uniqueness, finiteness, convergence, or finite-sample
inferential calibration.
