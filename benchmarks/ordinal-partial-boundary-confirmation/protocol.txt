Saturated logit boundary certificate: fresh deterministic confirmation
Frozen 2026-11-04 before numerical evaluation of this study

Claim and separation
--------------------
Confirm the established endpoint-half-count adjusted-score identity after an
invertible affine pattern-coordinate map, and the bounded detector implementing
its sufficient no-interior-root certificate. This is separate from development
of the sparse 96-row fixture; that fixture is not confirmation evidence and its
root-existence question remains unresolved. There are no inference simulations.

Fixed designs
-------------
Use three distinct two-dimensional patterns (.2,-.4),(1.1,.3),(-.5,.8), offsets
(.13,-.21,.08), weights (2,1,3), and count rows (4,0,3,2),(2,5,0,4),(1,3,2,5).
The global category frequencies are positive. Two intermediate pattern counts
are zero. The pattern matrix (1,-z) is square and full rank. Contrast this with
the same counts after replacing zeros by 1: its exact meanBR root is interior.

At three fixed interior coefficient vectors, compare the native full-model
adjusted scores with independent endpoint-half-count likelihood scores in
pattern coordinates, mapped affinely back to original coefficients (absolute
tolerance 2e-10). Map the exact closed-simplex boundary target to finite original
coefficients; verify its forced zero probabilities and adjacent predictor ties.
For the positive-count case, require native convergence at the existing
tol=1e-10 and all predicted probabilities equal the exact target within 2e-9.

Detector invariants
-------------------
The same certificate must result for grouped and coded-expanded data, source
blocks of 1 and 7 rows, and invertible centered/scaled predictor recoding. Zero
effective rows, including multiplication underflow, must not add patterns.
Decline certification for any common predictor, ML, other links, binary outcomes,
an additional varying pattern, an exactly different offset within a pattern,
or a rank-deficient pattern matrix (including a large affine location shift).
Endpoint-only missing cells and positive intermediate counts must not be
classified as this boundary case. Pattern state must never grow past p_n+1;
an extra pattern disables and releases it.

All failures are retained. Existing output is never replaced. Source hashes,
full per-check records and environment are saved. Numerical success validates
the implementation of the certificate; root absence in its stated saturated
logit scope follows from the separate exact derivation, not optimizer failure.
