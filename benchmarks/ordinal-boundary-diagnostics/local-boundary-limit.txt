Conditional local boundary analysis for the unchanged mean-bias equations
2026-11-04

Question and assumptions
------------------------
The fixed-gap study asks whether a stalled fit is approaching a genuine root
or a boundary point with a nonzero normal equation. This note derives a local
extension of the existing adjusted-score vector. It supplies no global
root-existence theorem and defines no replacement estimate.

Let theta be the original or affinely equivalent working coefficient vector.
At one input pattern, suppose a single intermediate category has probability
p(theta) = G(t + delta) - G(t), where delta = a' theta, t = x' theta - offset,
and the upper threshold's design vector is x + a. Its positive frequency total
is m, and its observed category count is zero. At the boundary point theta_0,
delta = 0 and t is finite. All other category probabilities, including those
at other positive-frequency input rows, remain positive. Assume G is smooth,
g = G' is positive, and the expected information J formed by excluding only
this vanishing category's contribution is positive definite at theta_0.
Logit and complementary log-log links satisfy the stated smoothness and
positive-density conditions at finite t.

Smooth inverse and adjusted score
---------------------------------
Write v = grad(p), H = J^(-1). At positive delta the full information and its
inverse satisfy the exact rank-one identities

 I = J + (m/p) v v',
 V = H - H v v' H / (p/m + v' H v).

At delta = 0, v = g a, so the inverse has the continuous extension

 V_0 = H - H a a' H / (a' H a),      V_0 a = 0.

All positive-category terms in the adjusted score are smooth at theta_0.
For the vanishing category the ordinary score contribution is zero because
its observed count is zero. Its adjusted count is

 c_delta = (m/2) [g'(t + delta) (x+a)' V (x+a) - g'(t) x' V x].

Here g' = G'' and g'' = G'''. Since V_0 a = 0, c_delta vanishes on the
boundary. Thus both p and c_delta have a factor delta locally, and c_delta/p
has a continuous extension. The apparently singular score contribution
v c_delta/p therefore also has a continuous extension. This uses a single
collapsing category; simultaneous distinct boundaries require another analysis.

An explicit limit useful for independent checking
------------------------------------------------
Let u = H a/(a' H a), s = x' V_0 x. For any smooth path with delta itself as
the path coordinate, differentiate V v = H v (p/m)/(p/m + v' H v). At zero,
v' along the path equals g' x plus a multiple of a. Consequently

 (dV/d delta) a = u/(m g) - (g'/g) V_0 x.

Differentiating c_delta gives

 C = lim c_delta/delta
   = [g'/(2g)] (2 x' u + 1)
     + (m/2) [g'' - 2(g')^2/g] s.

The vanishing category's limiting adjusted-score contribution is a C. Add this
to the ordinary and adjusted contributions of all positive categories, using
V_0 in their count corrections, to obtain the full continuous boundary score.
Keeping the rotation of grad(p) is essential: omitting it would lose the
-2(g')^2/g term and give the wrong limiting equation.

On a branch with exactly zero tangent score, write U* = lambda(delta) a. If
lambda approaches a nonzero finite value, I has leading term (m g/delta) a a'.
Every nonzero score component divided by sqrt(I_jj) is then O(sqrt(delta));
components with a_j = 0 have zero score. Also V U* = O(delta), by the inverse
expansion above. Thus those two numerical quantities can decrease together
without U* tending to zero. This statement does not remove the fitter's
additional conditioning check or establish a false-convergence event in it.

For checking derivatives, logit has
g' = g(1-2G), g'' = g[(1-2G)^2-2g]. Complementary log-log has
g' = g(1-exp(t)), g'' = g[1-3exp(t)+exp(2t)].

What this does and does not establish
------------------------------------
Under the assumptions above the full adjusted score extends continuously to
theta_0. If that exact limiting vector is nonzero, a sufficiently small
neighborhood of theta_0 contains no interior adjusted-score root. In particular,
a nonzero limiting normal component suffices. This is a local conditional
conclusion from continuity. It does not exclude another boundary point or any
root elsewhere in the admissible parameter domain.

The study numerically evaluates this limit at the two projected final points.
Exact rational checks on their binary64 inputs verify one collapsed gap, all
other positive gaps, zero observed frequency in the collapsed category, and a
full-rank set of three other varying-predictor patterns. The latter gives
positive-definite J: those three noncollapsed patterns alone identify all nine
fully varying coefficients. The score values still use 80-decimal arithmetic,
without a certified interval enclosure, and the projected points are numerical
approximations to the branch limits. Therefore no new root-absence certificate
is added to production.

The remaining global obligation is a no-zero result for U*(theta) over the
entire open polyhedron of strictly ordered observed-row cumulative predictors,
or an independently verified interior root. Neither finite-grid continuation
nor failed starts establishes it. Concavity of the ordinary log likelihood is
not a substitute: the full mean-bias adjustment need not be the gradient of a
scalar penalty, and adjusted counts can be negative.

Source and attribution
----------------------
The original global mean-bias equations are established: Kosmidis (2014),
Improved estimation in cumulative link models, JRSS B 76, 169-196,
DOI 10.1111/rssb.12025, equations (6)-(9), pp. 174-175. Section 5.1 explains
