Noncanonical binomial Firth performance: engineering confirmation
===============================================================

Baseline: c3b3eeb, the accurate-tail implementation committed before this work.
The performance objective is to remove duplicated arithmetic and allocations
while keeping the adjusted equations, stopping controls, covariance, likelihood,
and residual definitions. This is a numerical/software optimization, not a new
statistical procedure or an inference-calibration experiment.

Development and confirmation are separate. development/profile.R uses seed
113101 at n=12,000 and 100,000 to distinguish native fitting from finalization
and derivative requests. It informs the implementation and is not untouched
confirmation. Its baseline and first-candidate profiles are retained.

The fixed confirmation runner run.R uses seed 113201 on five designs:
  small: n=600, p=3 including intercept;
  ordinary: n=12,000, p=6;
  wide: n=12,000, p=20;
  large: n=120,000, p=6;
  grouped: n=12,000, p=6, binomial trial totals 4/9/20, frequency weights
           0/1/2/1, and varying location offsets.
Both probit and cloglog are fitted by QR and LLT. Ordinary logit is an unaffected
control. Each complete fit and derivative request is timed 11 times after a
warm-up. Both libraries run in separate R processes without competing numerical
or compilation jobs. The runner refuses to overwrite existing raw timings.

summarize.R records all cases, median speed ratios, coefficient/covariance and
post-fit differences, and whether iteration counts agree. The correctness
requirement is mathematical-equation identity, not a selected speed target.
The comparison requires identical iteration counts and numerical errors below
1e-9 (relative for extensive likelihood/deviance sums). Small summation-order
roundoff does not alter the estimand. Neither coefficients nor stopping rules
may be changed to achieve a speed target. Timing results are descriptive of
these designs and machine; they do not establish uniform speed dominance.

The previous 146-case independent adjusted-score panel is also rerun unchanged
in meaning, with outputs written under this study rather than replacing prior
evidence. Focused tests check count/weight conventions, zero-weight rows,
extreme tails, missing values, derivatives, and compact inference modes.

Final source hashes are to be frozen before confirmation results are read.
The archived source delta identifies the candidate independently of the package
version string. Session information and installed native-library hashes record
the environment. A separate baseline library and source archive remain immutable.

Reproduction from repository root:
  Rscript benchmarks/firth-tail-performance/run.R BASELINE_LIBRARY baseline RESULTS_DIRECTORY
  Rscript benchmarks/firth-tail-performance/run.R CANDIDATE_LIBRARY candidate RESULTS_DIRECTORY
  Rscript benchmarks/firth-tail-performance/summarize.R RESULTS_DIRECTORY baseline candidate
The profiling script accepts LIBRARY and OUTPUT_DIRECTORY separately.
